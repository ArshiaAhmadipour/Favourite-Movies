My Media Vault

This is my personal cinema sanctuary — a love letter to every movie, series, anime, and animation that has ever moved me, made me laugh, cry, or stay up all night thinking.

I’ve always kept messy text files and notes about what I’ve watched and how much I loved (or didn’t love) something. One day I got tired of that chaos and decided to build something beautiful, honest, and truly mine.

So here it is: every rating is 100% my own taste (yes, Rango and Surf’s Up are perfect 10s — fight me), every poster is pulled straight from TMDB, and every detail page is exactly how I want to remember these stories.

Built with Django, a lot of coffee, late-night debugging sessions, and an unhealthy obsession with perfect grid layouts. Deployed so I (and maybe you) can visit it anytime, anywhere.
